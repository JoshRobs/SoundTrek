# SoundTrek — Daily Marketing Routine (60 min)

---

## Minutes 0–15: Scan and Reply *(highest value — never skip this block)*

- [ ] Check Bluesky game music / indie dev feeds + any lists you've built of composers/VGM accounts
- [ ] Check Twitter notifications + search "game soundtrack," "VGM," or a specific composer name you're knowledgeable about
- [ ] Leave 3–5 genuine replies — add a real observation, correction, or lesser-known fact. No links in week 1–2.
- [ ] Once past the pure-engagement phase: 1 of those replies can naturally reference a specific Collection, not the homepage

---

## Minutes 15–25: Follow and Seed Your Feed

- [ ] Follow 5–10 new accounts/day — composers, VGM curators, indie devs in adjacent spaces
- [ ] Like/repost 2–3 things that are genuinely good, unrelated to self-promotion

---

## Minutes 25–45: One Piece of Content

Rotate through this fixed weekly schedule so you're never starting blank:

| Day | Format | What to post |
|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | Composer spotlight | Short post on an underrated composer — tag them if they're active |
| **Tue** | "Did you know" trivia | Something you discovered during the tagging work |
| **Wed** | Collection share | Post a specific Collection with 1–2 sentences on why you made it |
| **Thu** | Build-in-public | One thing you fixed or shipped that week — dev-facing audience |
| **Fri** | Ask a question | "What's the most underrated JRPG soundtrack?" — generates replies and surfaces names for future spotlights |
| **Weekend** | Skip or light engagement only | Reply-only is fine, don't force weekend content |

---

## Minutes 45–60: Log and Adjust

- [ ] Note which post/reply got any traction (reply, like, click) — 30 seconds, running note is fine
- [ ] **Once a week** (pick a fixed day): spend this slot reviewing what worked instead of posting — this is how the routine improves instead of just repeating

---

## Rules

**Phasing:**
- **Weeks 1–2:** Zero self-promotion. Pure engagement + the content rotation above. Posts should be about VGM generally, not about SoundTrek.
- **Week 3+:** Start weaving in Collection links naturally where relevant. Still value-first, self-promotion secondary.

**If a day gets busy:**
- Minutes 0–15 (replies) is the non-negotiable block. Everything else is droppable.

**Sustainability check:**
- Revisit in 3–4 weeks. If this hour isn't producing measurable referrer traffic or engagement growth, cut it back in favour of Reddit / Product Hunt / Hacker News — channels where a single well-crafted post fits your working style better.

---

## Platform Priority

1. **Bluesky** — more active VGM community right now, friendlier algorithm for new accounts, lead here
2. **Twitter** — follow and engage composers directly (many are active and reply); a composer amplifying their own page on SoundTrek is the highest-value outcome
